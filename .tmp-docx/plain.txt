
LOGISTICS MANAGEMENT PORTAL
PROJECT PROPOSAL & QUOTATION
1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the scope, deliverables, pricing, and payment terms for the development and customization of a Custom Logistics & Shipment Management Web Portal.
The proposed solution is a web-based business operations portal for managing orders, shipments, AWB assignment, pickup operations, reporting, and customer shipment tracking. The base system is already established, enabling faster delivery and cost-effective implementation compared to building from scratch.
